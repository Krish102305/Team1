## Goal
[00:00] The video demonstrates a workflow for creating and selling digital PDF guides online, using AI to generate the product content and marketing materials, and a standard e-commerce platform for sales.

## Prerequisites
- [02:53] PDF Trend Lab account
- [05:03] Claude AI account
- [05:27] Canva account (mentioned, interface not shown)
- [08:42] Shopify account
- [10:11] X (Twitter) account

## Steps
1. [02:55] In PDF Trend Lab, click "Generate Ideas".
2. [03:02] Select a niche from the dropdown (e.g., "Parenting").
3. [03:04] Click the "Generate ideas" button.
4. [04:52] Review the generated ideas and click "Save" on appealing ones.
5. [05:03] In Claude AI, enter a prompt instructing it to create a PDF guide based on the saved title and subtitle.
6. [05:38] Click the download icon to save the generated PDF.
7. [08:42] Sign up for a Shopify account to create a storefront.
8. [09:09] In the Shopify website editor, click "Add section".
9. [09:11] Click "Generate" and describe what you want to build to have AI create website sections.
10. [10:23] On X (Twitter), create relatable thread posts related to the guide's topic.
11. [11:47] Format the posts as a numbered list to increase engagement.
12. [12:03] Insert a call-to-action link to the PDF guide within the thread.
13. [12:46] (Optional) Copy a successful post, paste it into Claude AI, and ask it to generate multiple similar posts.

## Code and config
[05:06] Prompt entered into Claude AI:
```text
Create an entire pdf guide for this title and subtitle: The Neurodivergent Gentle Parenting Toolkit
Adapting gentle parenting techniques specifically for ADHD and autistic children
Make the title and subtitle short and punchy so it is easy to read and understand.
Design the entire pdf guide with the pages designed and exportable to canva.
```
