## Gotchas
*   [00:27] Income claims related to Whop clipping found online are often widely inflated.
*   [02:00] Whop clipping is not affiliate marketing; you are paid purely per view, not a commission on generated sales.
*   [03:00] Avoid joining campaigns where the budget is already mostly drained (e.g., 90% depleted), as you risk putting in effort without receiving payment once the budget hits zero.
*   [03:32] A legitimate clipping job will never ask you to pay a fee to join.
*   [03:36] Do not trust individuals or programs guaranteeing high earnings (e.g., $5,000) in your first month.
*   [06:45] Beginners who try to scale too quickly by managing multiple creators across multiple platforms on day one often burn out. It is recommended to start with one creator and scale up gradually.
